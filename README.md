# ABOUT MY FAVORITE KDRAMA SERIES
**Would You Marry Me?**
*Romantic comedy (with slice-of-life elements, a bit of family/business intrigue)*
> Yoo Me-ri, after her fiancé leaves and she’s hit by a housing / lease scam, ends up winning a “luxury newly-wed home” prize — but the condition is she must be married.
1. Choi Woo‑shik as Kim Woo-joo
2. Jung So‑min as Yoo Me-ri
- Writer: Lee Ha‑na (who has tackled romantic comedies before)
- Directors: Song Hyun‑wook & Hwang In‑hyeok
